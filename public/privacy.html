Your game. Your privacy.

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Guest play

Your game, unlocked levels, best scores, and preferences are stored in this browser. Clearing browser data removes guest progress. Guest play does not create a database account.

Google sign-in

Signing in uses Google’s basic identity service. We store your Google account identifier, first name, game progress, save version, and secure session records in Cloudflare D1. We do not store your Google password or request access to your mail, contacts, or files. Google verifies your identity; a secure, HttpOnly cookie keeps you signed in for up to 30 days.

Cards and gameplay clips

Share cards are generated on your device. Silent gameplay clips capture the game canvas only, using a short buffer in browser memory. We do not request access to your screen, microphone, or camera. We never upload cards or recordings to our database. Closing the tab removes the clip buffer. Files you download remain on your device. Sharing a file through your device’s share menu sends it only to the destination you choose.

Services

Cloudflare hosts this game and its account database. Google provides sign-in and fonts. These services receive the connection information necessary to serve requests. We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use this website through usage metrics, heatmaps, and session replays. Clarity collects interaction, device, and connection data and may use cookies and similar technologies. Microsoft processes this data to provide Clarity and for its own purposes, as described in the Microsoft Privacy Statement. Clarity cannot capture the contents of the game canvas; gameplay clips remain on your device as described above.

Your control

You can sign out at any time. To permanently remove your game account, sign in, open your player profile below the board, and choose Delete game account. This deletes your profile, game save, and all game sessions. It does not affect your Google account. Guest progress in your browser is kept separately.
